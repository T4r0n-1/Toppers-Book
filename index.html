Yes. The planned release is one complete chapterwise book for Mathematics Standard (041). It will go on sale only after all chapters and solutions are completed and checked.

Can I buy the book right now?

Not yet. The book is in preparation. The ₹199 regular price and ₹149 coupon price are planned launch prices. We are not accepting advance payments.

Can I download or print the book?

This is an online reading product. There is no PDF download or print option. You will need to sign in and have an internet connection to read it.

How long will my access last?

Access lasts 365 days from successful payment. The expiry date will appear in My Books. This is a one-time purchase, with no automatic renewal.

Why will my name and mobile number appear?

Each purchased copy is personalised to discourage sharing. Your name and the mobile number you provide at checkout will appear on reading pages. We will disclose this before purchase.

The reader is designed for phones, tablets and computers. You can zoom into pages, navigate between chapters and resume from your last reading position.
