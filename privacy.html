Information we use

When you create an account or make a purchase, we use your sign-in email and identifier, reader name, mobile number, purchase and payment references, and access expiry to provide your account and purchased books. Google sign-in may also provide your profile name and picture. We use browser storage to keep sign-in state and your reading position.

Personalised reading pages

Your name and mobile number will appear in a watermark on your purchased reading pages to discourage redistribution. This will be disclosed before payment. Sharing a screenshot can reveal these details, so keep your reading pages private.

Service providers

We use Cloudflare for hosting and protected content. Firebase Authentication handles Google and email/password sign-in. Passwords are handled by Firebase; we do not store them in our own database. Razorpay processes payments. These providers process information under their own policies. We do not store card numbers, CVV or UPI PINs.

Retention and your requests

We retain account and purchase information as needed to provide access, resolve issues and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. Contact us to request correction or deletion. Some transaction records may need to be retained even after account deletion. We do not sell your personal information.

Students and parents

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to manage the purchase and provide appropriate account and contact details.
